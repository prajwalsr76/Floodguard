Where alerts go

Get alerts on your phone (Telegram, free)

Takes about two minutes, once.

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot and choose any name, for example "FloodGuard Mahanadi". Copy the token it gives you.
  2. Paste the token here and press Connect.
  3. Open your new bot from the link that appears, press Start, then press Connect again. Everyone who presses Start gets the alerts, so family, friends or a whole Telegram group can join.

The token is saved only in config/alerts.json on this computer. E-mail and SMS can be added in the same file (docs/ALERTS_SETUP.md).

Alert levels

LevelRule, on tomorrow's forecast at any of the three gauges
NormalMore than 0.5 m below the warning level, and the 80% range stays below it
WatchWithin 0.5 m of the warning level, or the 80% range reaches it
WarningAt or above the warning level
DangerAt or above the danger level
Data delayedA forecast gauge has sent no reading for more than 6 hours: the level is not updated and no "all clear" is sent

Limitations

FloodGuard is a final-year research prototype, not an official warning. Always follow the Central Water Commission (CWC), OSDMA and district administration. Water levels: CWC. Rain: CHIRPS and Open-Meteo. Radar: Copernicus Sentinel-1. Terrain and land cover: SRTM, ESA WorldCover, JRC. Places: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
